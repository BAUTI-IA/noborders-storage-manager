1The 3 ideas that put everything in order

Understand this page and the rest is just pressing buttons.

💡 Idea 1 — A charge and a payment are different things

A charge is something the client owes: the job balance (pickup + delivery), the BOL balance, every extra / additional service, and each month of storage billing. A payment is money that came in. The magic is connecting them: every payment is applied against one or more charges. That connection is the allocation ("Assign to charges").

💡 Idea 2 — The client pays ONE amount, the job has SEVERAL charges

The client doesn't pay "the shuttle" and separately "the balance" — they hand over $1,500 all together. If you enter that as a loose payment, the system can't tell what got paid: the job may look "collected" with an unpaid extra (and the driver's commission stuck), or the other way around. That's why you always allocate.

💡 Idea 3 — Whatever you don't allocate stays "On account" (not an error… if you resolve it)

If money comes in and you don't know yet what it's for, it stays "On account": received but not applied. It isn't lost — the job shows it as "On account (unallocated)" with an Asignar / Assign button to spread it later. Golden rule: by the end of the day, nothing on account.

The journey of every dollar

Charge pending→ Payment entered
+ Payment→ Allocated to its charges
✂️ Assign→ In circulation
if cash / check→ Deposited 🏦
💳 Method rule: digital payments (Zelle, Venmo, card, wire, PayPal, Cash App, Apple Pay) are marked deposited automatically. Physical ones (cash, checks, money orders) stay in circulation — someone is literally holding that money — until you deposit them.

3Entering a payment, step by step 💰

Two doors, same result: "+ Payment" on the Payments page, or "+ Add payment" on the job (fastest — job, balance and driver come pre-loaded).

1Job. Search by number or client and pick it. If the job has pending extras, the allocation panel arms itself (section 4).
2Date and amount. The payment date and the total that came in (everything the client handed over, together).
3Concept. Job / Extra / CC Fee / Storage / On account / Other. If you're going to split across charges, don't sweat this — the allocation rules.
4Discount (optional) + reason. Net = amount − discount, shown live.
5Stage: At pick up / At delivery / Other — informational only.
6Method. Cash, credit card, Zelle, Venmo, check, money order, PayPal, Cash App, wire, Apple Pay. Method-specific fields open up (section 7): check/MO serial + photo, or the card's CC fee.
7Received: by whom and when (digital methods tick themselves). For physical money: "Who has the money?" — pre-filled with the job's driver. Never leave it empty: it feeds the "In circulation" view.
8Deposited already? If the cash/check already hit the bank, tick deposited + date + bank account. If not, it stays in circulation (batch deposit later).
9Allocate with "✂️ Assign to charges" (section 4) and save — the button reads Create payment (or Create split payments if you split).
🧾 The payment composer (from the job's Overview tab). The Job total card's + Add payment works line by line: each row is a Concept + Method + amount, and "+ Another payment method" adds more rows — so "$800 cash + $700 Zelle" (or "balance by check + the extra in cash") is saved as separate payment rows in one shot, with one date and note, and a running Total at the bottom.
🔎 "Does this job have payments?" Search a job number in Payments: a banner answers instantly — ✅ green with count, total and received; 🚫 red "No payments recorded"; or ⚠️ amber if no job matches that number.

4Allocation: "✂️ Assign to charges"

Split ONE payment across the job's charges, line by line. Thirty seconds that keep everything true.

1In the payment modal, tick "✂️ Assign to charges (split)". The purple panel opens: "Split the payment between the job balance and the pending extras. Whatever you don't assign stays on account."
2One line appears per charge: the job balance and every active extra, each with its "Pending: $X" (what that charge is still owed).
3The system already proposes the split: job balance first, then extras oldest-first. You can override any line by hand.
4Each line shows "Remaining: $Y" after your allocation — you see live what every charge still owes.
5The footer shows "Unassigned (on account): $… / Total: $…" with ✓ or ✗. The ideal: $0 unassigned.

The panel's rules (what will happen to you)

  • Went over on a line? Allowed: it shows "Overpayment $Z — recorded anyway" (useful when the client overpaid one specific charge).
  • Lines add up to more than the payment? That's blocked: an error tells you the allocations exceed the total — lower a line.
  • Money left unassigned? It automatically stays on account (section 5). Not an error — but remember to assign it.
  • A charge is missing? "+ New extra / another line" creates the charge on the spot: pick the concept (Job / Extra CF / Shuttle / Long carry / Stairs / Packing…), amount and note — and that extra is born straight into the commission system (section 6).
✅ Why it's worth it: with the payment allocated, the job tells the truth — balance settled, packing paid, long carry owing $50 — and that's what the Dispatching labels (Outstanding / Not collected), the trips' "To collect", the job's Outstanding pill and the driver commissions all read. An unallocated payment leaves all of that lying.

5"On account": money that came in but wasn't applied

Received ✓ · applied ✗. Fixed with the Assign button.

  • How it happens: you entered a payment with concept "On account", or you allocated and left a leftover unassigned.
  • Where you see it: in the job detail, Money tab → Payments block: "On account (unallocated): $X" with the Asignar button. Also in the Payments table.
  • How to apply it: tap Asignar → the payment reopens with the amount locked and the allocation panel forced on, pre-spread over what the job still owes. Adjust if needed and save.
⚠️ Golden rule: "on account" is a transitional state. Money on account = charges still showing unpaid and commissions stuck. Before closing the day, check nothing is left unassigned.

6Extras: create them, collect them, commission them ➕

An extra (additional service) is an added charge on a job — shuttle, long carry, packing… — that also splits a commission between driver and rep.

The 4 ways to create an extra

1. The job's services stripThe Additional services strip at the top of the job detail — anyone can add one; amounts edit inline, ✎ opens description + generated by + %.The moment the service is agreed with the client — it's always in sight.
2. The job's Money tabExtras section → "+ Add extra": type, amount, date, driver, generated by, rep, %.Full control over every field, including the reporting month.
3. The Extras pageEach job shows the 7 types as rows — tick the box and fill in amount and commissions.Bulk load / end-of-week review per driver.
4. A payment's splitIn "✂️ Assign to charges" → "+ New extra / another line": concept + amount.The client is already paying for something never entered — create and collect in one step.

What every extra carries

  • Type: Extra CF · Shuttle · Long carry · Stairs · Packing · Flight charge · Other.
  • Amount, date ("Month this extra is reported and paid in" — defaults to today; commissions report under this month, not the job's month), the driver who generated it (defaults to the job's), "Generated by" (Driver only / Driver + Rep / Rep only), the rep if involved, and the commission % — auto-filled by these rules, editable per case:

How an extra gets COLLECTED (here's the tricky part)

1The extra exists → it appears as its own line in the allocation panel of any payment on that job, with its "Pending: $X".
2When you assign money to it, the extra turns Paid (green chip on the job). Partial payment = still Pending for the rest.
3Extra collected → its commission is locked in and shows in the yellow COMMISSION row of the driver/rep on the Extras page — drivers and reps now share the same table layout (Date · Type · Amount · Generated by · % · Commission) — ready to send with 📋 Copy or 🖨️ PDF.
🚫 The classic mistake: charging the client "all together" and entering the payment without allocating. Result: the job looks over-collected, the extras stay "Pending", the driver chases his commission and nobody understands anything. A payment that includes extras is ALWAYS entered with the split.
💡 Note: when a split creates a new extra, its commission is then assigned with the "Assign commission" modal (generated by / driver % / rep %) — the system shows the driver / rep / company split live. Long carry and stairs come with the driver fixed at 50%. And remember: every service added or edited on the job posts a note with who and when — the trail can only be deleted by the dispatch manager.

7Methods with paperwork: check, money order, card 🧾

The three methods that ask for extra data. Enter it complete — it's your backup if something goes missing.

🏦 Check

  • Type: Cashier's / Personal / Official.
  • Serial number — the system warns live if that number is already entered (duplicate check).
  • Bank/issuer, date, memo (and routing/last 4 for personal checks).
  • 📷 Photo of the check — always take it; it stays on the row (Photo column).

📮 Money order

  • Type: USPS / Western Union / MoneyGram / Other.
  • Serial (also duplicate-checked), date, office, purchaser.
  • 📷 Photo of the money order.

💳 Credit card (CC fee)

Tick "Charge CC fee to client" (3% by default): the system computes the fee and the total to collect, and on save creates a second payment with concept CC Fee by itself. Don't enter it manually — it duplicates. Collected fees show in the purple Payments metric.

👐 Cash, checks and money orders stay IN CIRCULATION. The "Who has the money?" field says who's carrying them. In the In circulation tab you see them grouped by person, with the days held: 💬 Request deposit sends the pre-written WhatsApp, and the batch deposit (tick → account → date → ✓ Deposit) marks them all deposited in one shot. The red alert lists whatever is 7+ days undeposited: that list must stay empty.
📒 Where do I see everything still owed? AP / AR. The AP / AR page (Finance menu, between Payments and Expenses) is the bird's-eye view: They owe us (AR) — job outstanding balances, storage billing, settlements in our favor — vs We owe (AP) — bills, expenses, settlements against us, driver pay weeks — with aging bars (0-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+) and one-click jumps back to each job, sheet or bill. If your allocation habits are clean, AR here is the truth.

8Recipes: what to do in every typical case 🍳

Find your case and follow the steps. These 8 cover 99% of the days.

1 · The client pays exactly the job balance (the easy one)

Job detail → + Add payment (job, balance and driver pre-loaded) → method → save. If the job has no extras, no need to open the split: the whole payment goes to the balance.

2 · Pays EVERYTHING together: balance + extras

+ Payment → total amount → ✂️ Assign to charges → the proposal already splits (balance first, extras after) → check the "Remaining" per line → save as Create split payments. Every extra turns Paid and the commissions lock in.

3 · Pays with two methods at once (cash + Zelle)

Job → Overview → Job total card → + Add payment → one line per method ("+ Another payment method") with its concept and amount → Save payment. Each line becomes its own payment row — the cash goes to circulation, the Zelle auto-deposits.

4 · Pays an extra that was NEVER entered

+ Payment → ✂️ Assign to charges → + New extra / another line → pick the concept (shuttle, packing…), amount and note → save. Then complete the commission with "Assign commission" (generated by / driver % / rep %).

5 · A deposit / advance "for the job in general"

+ Payment → concept "On account" (or leave the leftover unassigned in the split) → when it's clear what it's for, Asignar button on the job → the panel opens pre-spread over what's owed → confirm.

6 · Pays less (partial) or pays more

Partial: enter what came in → ✂️ split → the proposal fills the job balance first; the "Remaining" numbers say exactly what's still owed. Overpay: assign the overpayment to its line ("Overpayment $Z — recorded anyway") or leave the excess on account for a future charge.

7 · Collecting the BOL of a broker delivery

Type it once — in the job form's "BOL collected + method + date" fields, in the job's Carrier Settlement section (Record payment), or on the closing sheet in Settlements. All three mirror into Payments automatically: one record, never two.

8 · Monthly storage payment

Storage Billing page → the cycle's charge is generated automatically → Mark paid (or enter the payment with concept Storage and allocate it). The 💬 WhatsApp reminder comes pre-written.

Cheat sheet 📌

  • A payment = what came in. The allocation = what it was for. Never enter the first without the second.
  • The job's balances are born in the form — pickup balance + delivery balance. Type them right.
  • The ✂️ panel already proposes the split — just check the "Remaining" numbers.
  • Charge missing? + New extra / another line, right there.
  • Money left over → on account → Asignar button before closing the day.
  • Extra collected = commission locked. Extra pending = driver chasing you. Extra's date = its reporting month.
  • BOL: once — the form, the job and Settlements all talk to Payments.
  • Physical money → who holds it → batch deposit → red alert at $0.

Weird case? Ask in the general channel in Chats before improvising. 💜
