NO BORDERS MOVING - CUSTOMER POLICIES AND FAQ

WHO WE ARE
No Borders Moving is an interstate (long-distance) household goods carrier. We pick up, transport, store and deliver household goods with our own trucks, and we run warehouses in Indiana and New Jersey. Many moves are sold by a moving broker or partner company; in that case the broker issued the estimate and No Borders is the carrier that performs the move. Questions about the original sales contract go to the company that sold it. Anything about the shipment itself - pickup, transit, storage, delivery and the balance owed to the carrier - is handled by No Borders.

YOUR JOB NUMBER
Every move has a job number. It is on your estimate, order for service and bill of lading. Before sharing anything about a shipment, our customer line asks for the job number plus the ZIP code of the pickup or delivery address, or the last 4 digits of the phone number on the job. This protects your information, so we cannot make exceptions - not for family members, and not for anyone calling on the customer's behalf.

WHAT EACH SHIPMENT STATUS MEANS
- Scheduled: the pickup is booked; nothing has been picked up yet.
- Picked up: your goods are loaded on our truck.
- In storage: your goods are in one of our warehouses or a storage facility, waiting for delivery.
- Out for delivery: your goods are on the truck that will deliver them.
- Delivered: the shipment has been delivered.
- On hold: a coordinator needs to review something before the shipment moves; the coordinator explains the reason.

DELIVERY DATES
- FADD means First Available Delivery Date: the first day we are able to deliver. It is not a promised delivery day.
- Your delivery day is scheduled by our dispatch team, within the delivery window on your paperwork, and confirmed with you before the truck arrives.
- Long-distance trucks carry several customers' shipments on one route, so the exact day depends on the route. If your delivery has not been scheduled yet, our dispatch team can call you back to confirm it.
- Someone must be at the delivery address to receive the shipment and sign the paperwork. If nobody is available, the shipment may have to go into storage, which can add charges.

PAYMENT
- The remaining balance is collected by our crew: any amount due at pickup is paid at pickup, and the balance due at delivery is paid before unloading.
- Have your payment ready before the truck arrives. Accepted methods: cash, Zelle, credit card, money order and cashier's check. Our crew confirms the exact options when they call ahead.
- Our customer line never takes card numbers or payments over the phone.
- If you were given a non-binding estimate, federal rules let the carrier require up to 110 percent of that estimate at delivery; any remaining charges are billed afterwards, due 30 days after delivery.
- Extra services added during the move (for example an additional stop, a long carry, stairs or re-delivery) can change the balance. Ask for a callback if you have questions about a specific charge.

STORAGE
- If your goods are in storage with us, the monthly storage rate in your agreement applies, billed monthly. Some agreements include the first month free.
- Storage rent that is past due must be paid before the shipment is released for delivery.
- To schedule delivery out of storage, or to ask about your storage bill, our customer line logs a request and a coordinator calls you back.

CHANGING YOUR MOVE
- Changes to the delivery date, delivery address, pickup date or contact information are requests, not instant changes. A coordinator reviews every request against the truck route and calls you back to confirm it.
- A change can affect your price, for example when the new address adds distance or the shipment has to be redelivered.
- Our customer line gives you a reference number for every request.

DAMAGE OR MISSING ITEMS
- At delivery, check your items and note any visible damage or missing items on the delivery paperwork before signing.
- Under federal rules you have 9 months from delivery to file a written claim. The carrier must acknowledge your claim within 30 days and pay, deny or make a settlement offer within 120 days.
- Our customer line logs your claim and a coordinator contacts you with the claim process. Keep damaged items and packaging until the claim is resolved.
- If a claim cannot be settled, federal rules require carriers to offer arbitration; for claims of 10,000 dollars or less, arbitration is binding if you request it.

QUOTES FOR A NEW MOVE
Our customer line does not give prices. It takes your name, phone number, origin and destination and an approximate move date, and a coordinator calls you back with a quote.

REACHING A PERSON
Our customer line can log an urgent or regular callback request for anything it cannot resolve: refunds, billing disputes, complaints, damage claims or anything else. You get a reference number, and a coordinator calls you back.
