Privacy Policy

NBM Driver is a work app for the drivers and crews of No Borders Moving and Storage. It is used by our employees and contractors to see their assigned jobs and record what happens in the field. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what we never do with it.

An account is required. NBM Driver only works with an account issued by No Borders Moving. It is not a consumer app and does not offer sign-up to the general public.

What the app collects

  • Your account — the email address and password you use to sign in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Job information you enter — measured cubic feet, lot numbers and sticker colors, job status, balances to collect, addresses, and notes about the move.
  • An edit history — for each change, we record which field changed, the new value, the time, and the account that made it. This exists so a mistake in a money field can be traced and corrected.

What the app does not collect

  • No background or continuous location tracking. The app never follows where you are.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or calendar.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, and no third-party analytics or tracking software.
  • No payment card data is stored on the device.

Address lookup

When you type an address, the app may send that partial text to Google Places to suggest matching addresses and fill in the ZIP, state and city. Only the address text is sent — never your account or your device location. This feature is optional and the app works without it.

Where the information lives

Job data is stored on your device so the app keeps working without signal, and is synced to our database hosted with Supabase on servers in the United States. Access is restricted so that each account can only reach the jobs it is entitled to see.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. Information is shared only with the service providers that make the app run: Supabase (database and authentication), Google (address lookup, when used), and Expo (app delivery and updates). Each processes it solely to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Job records are business records of completed moves and are retained as long as our operations and accounting obligations require. If your employment or contract with No Borders Moving ends, your account is deactivated and you lose access to the app.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information tied to your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Write to the contact address below and we will respond. Job records that are part of a completed move may be kept where we are legally required to do so, even after an account is deleted.

Children

NBM Driver is a workplace tool and is not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify account holders.

Common questions

I can't sign in. Use the same email and password you use for the No Borders CRM. If you don't have an account yet, ask the office to create one for you — accounts cannot be created from the app.

I don't see any jobs. Jobs appear once the office assigns them to you. If your account was just created, the office may still need to link it to your driver record.

I have no signal on the road. Keep working normally. Everything you enter is saved on the phone, and the bar at the top of the screen tells you what is still waiting to upload. It syncs by itself when coverage returns.

A number I entered looks wrong in the office system. Every change is recorded with the time and the account that made it, so the office can trace and correct it. Tell them the job number and what you expected to see.
